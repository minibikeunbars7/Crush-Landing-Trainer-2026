## 🎮 About the Game

Released on August 31, 2026, Sucker for Love: Crush Landing is a horror-comedy visual novel developed by Akabaka and published by Black Lantern Collective. It combines romance, Lovecraftian horror, point-and-click exploration, color-based puzzles, and multiple story endings.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sucker for Love: Crush Landing**. This trainer provides quality-of-life and progression options for managing Hheily's fading flame, handling the apartment's timed events, preserving useful items, and exploring alternate choices without repeatedly restarting scenes.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to focus on the story, experiment with Hheily's color-based feeding system, revisit branching choices, or reduce the pressure of timed apartment events.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Event Timer** — Stops the countdown governing major story events, giving you more time to explore, inspect objects, answer the door, and make calls.
- **Restore Hheily's Flame** — Restores Hheily's flame and prevents it from being extinguished while you solve color-based feeding puzzles.
- **Pause Visitor Events** — Delays door and visitor events so you can finish examining the apartment or preparing the next offering.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Item Uses** — Prevents selected apartment objects and collected offerings from being consumed when used to feed Hheily.

### 📌 Puzzle

- **Lock Item Colors** — Preserves the current color state of applicable objects, helping you plan offerings without losing access to needed color categories.

### 📌 Story

- **Choice Checkpoint** — Creates a convenient restore point before important dialogue and interaction choices.
- **Unlock Ending Routes** — Provides progression assistance for revisiting branching scenes and reaching alternate outcomes.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore the hand-drawn, fully navigable apartment by moving between rooms and examining interactive objects. The trainer does not replace normal navigation; it reduces time pressure while you search the environment.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect useful objects around the apartment and use them as offerings for Hheily's flame. Because items and color options can be limited, enable Unlimited Item Uses or Lock Item Colors before experimenting with different feeding orders.

### World & Environment

The apartment is the main exploration space, with interactive furniture, containers, changing objects, telephone conversations, letters, and visitors at the door. Freeze the event timer when you want to inspect every area without advancing the story clock.

### Combat & Weapons

Crush Landing is not built around conventional combat. Its danger comes from timed events, story choices, supernatural encounters, and keeping Hheily's flame alive. Use Restore Hheily's Flame and Pause Visitor Events to manage these threats without skipping the investigation.
